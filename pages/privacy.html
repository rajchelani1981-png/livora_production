Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026 · Version 1.0

This Privacy Policy explains how Livora Production Private Limited ("Livora", "we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, stores and shares personal information when you use the Livora website, mobile application and related live-streaming and social features (together, the "Services"). Please read it carefully.

1. Who we are and how to contact us

Livora Production Private Limited operates the Livora Services. Privacy questions or requests can be sent to support@livoraproduction.com

2. Information we collect

Account and profile information. When you register or use an account, we may collect your mobile number, email address, name, username, profile photo, gender and date of birth, as well as information needed to authenticate your sign-in through mobile, email, Google or Apple.

User content and communications. We process profile photos, other photos and videos you upload, livestream audio/video, party-room voice audio, chat messages and related information such as the room, account and time associated with the activity.

Location and device information. With the relevant device permission, we may collect precise GPS location. We may also derive or process approximate location and location information from your IP address. We may receive technical information such as device, app, network and diagnostic data needed to operate, secure and troubleshoot the Services.

Support and safety information. If you contact support, submit a report, block another user or participate in a moderation review, we process the information you provide and information needed to investigate and address the issue.

3. Camera, microphone, photos and notifications

Livora requests camera and microphone access when you use features such as livestreaming or party rooms. Photo-library access is used when you choose to select or upload a profile photo or other media. Location permission supports location-related functionality. Notifications may be used to deliver account, room, social and service alerts. You can manage these permissions through your device settings; disabling a permission may make the related feature unavailable.

4. Livestreams, party rooms and chat

Livestreams and party-room voice conversations are recorded and stored. Chat messages are also stored. We retain livestream recordings, party-room voice recordings and chat messages for one month. These records may be used to provide the Services, support moderation and safety investigations, respond to reports, enforce our rules and address legal or security issues. Access is limited to purposes connected with operating and protecting the Services.

5. How long we keep other content

Profile photos are retained until you replace them or delete your account. Other photos and videos you upload are retained for one month. We may retain limited information for longer where necessary to comply with law, resolve disputes, enforce our terms, investigate abuse or protect users and the Services. Where information is retained for such reasons, it will be handled for those limited purposes.

6. How we use information

We use information to create and manage accounts; authenticate users; display profiles; provide livestreaming, party rooms, chat and other social features; process location-related features you choose to use; send service notifications; provide customer support; detect, investigate and address fraud, misuse and policy violations; moderate content and respond to reports; maintain, secure and improve the Services; and comply with applicable legal obligations.

7. How information may be shared

Information may be visible to other users according to the features and settings you use. For example, your profile details and content you choose to share may be displayed to viewers or room participants. We may also share information with service providers that help us operate, secure, support or deliver the Services, but only as reasonably needed for their services; with moderators or authorised personnel handling safety and support; with legal, regulatory or law-enforcement authorities when required or legally permitted; and in connection with a corporate reorganisation, subject to appropriate safeguards. We do not state that all information remains exclusively within Livora.

8. Your choices and privacy rights

You can update profile information through the app where those controls are available, manage device permissions in your device settings, and use the in-app reporting and blocking features. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. Contact support@livoraproduction.com to make a privacy request. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

9. Account deletion

You can initiate account deletion from inside the Livora app using its account-deletion feature. Account deletion may make your profile and account unavailable. We may retain limited information where required or permitted by law, or where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, enforcement of our terms or the handling of safety reports. Content already seen, copied or captured by other users may remain outside our control.

10. Age restriction

Livora is intended only for people who are at least 18 years old. You must not create or use an account if you are under 18. If we become aware that an underage person has created an account, we may take steps to restrict or terminate the account and address the associated information in accordance with applicable law.

11. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and organisational measures designed to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or alteration. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. International processing

Information may be processed in locations where Livora or its service providers operate. The specific locations and safeguards depend on the services and providers used. Where applicable law requires safeguards for international transfers, we will take steps required by that law.

13. Changes to this Policy

We may revise this Policy as our Services or legal requirements change. We will publish the revised version on this page and update the effective date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or seek consent.
